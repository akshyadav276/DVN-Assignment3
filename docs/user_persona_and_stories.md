# User Persona And Stories

## Stakeholder Hat

Federal Transport Minister reviewing national road safety funding priorities.

## Persona

The stakeholder needs a concise, evidence-led view of where national road-safety intervention should be prioritised. They need to understand national scale, current urgency, fair state-level risk, repeatable fatality patterns, and the potential impact of targeted action.

## User Stories

| User Story | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|
| As the Federal Transport Minister, I want to see whether fatalities are improving over time so that I can understand national policy urgency. | Dashboard includes a historical fatality trend and latest-period KPI. |
| As the Federal Transport Minister, I want fair state comparison so that national funding priorities are not interpreted only through raw fatality counts. | Dashboard includes fatalities per 100,000 people. |
| As the Federal Transport Minister, I want to identify repeatable risk patterns so that interventions can be targeted by road environment and road-user group. | Dashboard shows time, remoteness, speed, and road-user patterns. |
| As the Federal Transport Minister, I want to test intervention impact so that I can justify national funding action. | Dashboard includes a what-if lives-saved parameter. |

## Definition Of Done

The dashboard is complete when the Federal Transport Minister can:

- see the national warning signal and latest intervention window without reading technical notes
- compare states using population-adjusted risk, not raw counts alone
- identify the two priority theatres that explain 70.8% of recent deaths
- understand that each theatre harms different road-user groups
- use the what-if control to estimate potential lives saved under a selected reduction target
- access clear data provenance, limitations, credits, and a data dictionary in the portfolio
