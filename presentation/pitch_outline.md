# Pitch Outline

## Title

The Preventable Pattern: Two Road-Safety Problems Hidden in One National Number

## Stakeholder Hat

Australian Transport Minister / Road Safety Funding Committee.

## Narrative Arc

The Detective.

## 5-Minute Flow

1. The assumption: road deaths feel random.
2. The evidence: historical progress has stalled.
3. The reveal: raw counts mislead; population-adjusted risk changes priority.
4. The pattern: regional high-speed vehicle-occupant deaths and urban arterial vulnerable-road-user deaths.
5. The decision: targeted intervention can estimate lives saved.
