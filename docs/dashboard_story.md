# Dashboard Story

## Title

The Everyday Fatality Pattern: Where Australia should target road safety funding first

## Core Thesis

Australia's road fatalities have fallen since 1989, but recent progress has stalled. The latest data shows that deaths cluster into two intervention patterns:

1. Regional high-speed vehicle-occupant fatalities.
2. Major-city urban-street vulnerable-road-user fatalities.

The dashboard helps a Federal Transport Minister reviewing national road safety funding priorities decide which road environments should be prioritised first across Australia.

## Narrative Arc

What -> So What -> What Next.

## Dashboard Flow

1. Historical scale and recent stall.
2. Raw counts versus population-adjusted risk.
3. Focus-area concentration into two priority theatres.
4. Human impact and timing patterns.
5. What-if lives-saved decision panel.
